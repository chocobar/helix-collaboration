# Design: Tomato Soup Recipe

## Overview

A classic homemade tomato soup recipe. Simple, comforting, and made with common pantry ingredients.

## Recipe

**Prep time:** 10 minutes | **Cook time:** 30 minutes | **Serves:** 4

### Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (28 oz / 800g) whole peeled tomatoes
- 1 cup vegetable or chicken broth
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon dried basil
- 1/4 teaspoon dried oregano
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1/4 cup heavy cream (optional)

### Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add diced onion and cook until soft and translucent, about 5 minutes.
3. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute, stirring frequently.
4. Pour in the canned tomatoes (with juices) and broth. Add sugar, salt, pepper, basil, and oregano.
5. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer uncovered for 20 minutes.
6. Remove from heat. Use an immersion blender to puree until smooth (or transfer in batches to a countertop blender — leave the lid slightly ajar to vent steam).
7. Stir in butter until melted. Add cream if using.
8. Taste and adjust salt and pepper as needed.
9. Serve hot, optionally with crusty bread or a grilled cheese sandwich.

### Tips

- For a richer flavor, roast fresh tomatoes (halved, drizzled with olive oil, 400F/200C for 25 min) instead of using canned.
- A pinch of red pepper flakes adds a nice subtle heat.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for 3-4 days or freeze for up to 3 months.

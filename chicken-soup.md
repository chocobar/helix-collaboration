# Classic Chicken Soup

**Prep time:** 20 minutes
**Cook time:** 45 minutes
**Servings:** 6

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 carrots, peeled and sliced
- 3 celery stalks, sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 8 cups chicken broth
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 lb (450g) boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 2 cups egg noodles (or pasta of choice)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion, carrots, and celery. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables begin to soften.
2. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Pour in the chicken broth. Add bay leaves and thyme. Bring to a boil.
4. Add the chicken breasts whole. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
5. Remove the chicken and shred it using two forks. Set aside.
6. Return the pot to a boil. Add the egg noodles and cook for 8 minutes, or until tender.
7. Add the shredded chicken back to the pot. Season with salt and pepper.
8. Remove bay leaves. Ladle into bowls and garnish with fresh parsley.

## Tips

- **Richer broth:** Use a mix of chicken thighs and breasts for deeper flavor.
- **Make ahead:** The soup keeps well in the fridge for up to 4 days. Add noodles when reheating to prevent them from getting mushy.
- **Variations:** Swap egg noodles for rice, orzo, or tortellini. Add a squeeze of lemon juice before serving for brightness.
- **Freezing:** Freeze without noodles for up to 3 months. Cook noodles fresh when serving.
